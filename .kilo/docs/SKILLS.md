### **2.1 Rotorcraft Flight Maneuvers & Handling Standards**

Pilots must demonstrate proficiency in managing complex aerodynamic phenomena:

> * **Effective Translational Lift (ETL) Transition:** Smooth management of collective power and cyclic trim while transitioning through 16\\text{ kts} to 24\\text{ kts} airspeed.  
> * **Vortex Ring State (VRS) Recovery:** Early recognition of high descent rates at low airspeeds (\> 500\\text{ fpm} descent below 30\\text{ kts}) and immediate execution of Vuichard or traditional recovery techniques.  
> * **Loss of Tail Rotor Effectiveness (LTE) Prevention:** Maintaining anti-torque control awareness during high-power, low-airspeed operations in unfavorable wind conditions.

### **2.2 Avionics, CPDS, and AFCS Mastery**

Pilots operating Eurocopter EC135, EC145, and Airbus H135 airframes are assessed on their interaction with cockpit automation:

> * **First Limit Indicator (FLI) Monitoring:** Continuous parameter tracking to prevent exceeding torque (TRQ), Turbine Outlet Temperature (TOT), or gas generator speed (N\_1) thresholds.  
> * **4-Axis Flight Control System:** Effective utilization of Stability Augmentation System (SAS) actuators, Auto Trim, and Beep Trim hat-switch inputs for workload reduction during instrument flight and low-visibility approaches.

In accordance with flight instruction standards (e.g., EASA AMC1 FCL.920), instructors within the Virtual HEMS ecosystem must master specific instructional competencies to facilitate effective learning.

### **3.1 Principles of Learning & Knowledge Retention**

Instructors apply key educational principles to maximize student retention and skill transfer:

> * **Known to Unknown:** Lessons progress sequentially from simple, familiar tasks to complex HEMS mission scenarios.  
> * **Active Engagement:** Trainees are actively engaged in hands-on practice as early as possible in each lesson.  
> * **Chunking & Short-Term Memory:** Complex tasks are broken down into manageable steps to respect human working memory limits (7 \\pm 2 items).  
> * **Threat & Error Management (TEM):** Embeds proactive hazard identification and countermeasure planning into every pre-flight briefing.

## **4\. Performance Assessment & Rating Scales**

Pilot handling qualities and maneuver precision are evaluated using standardized objective and subjective rating criteria.  
`+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+`  
`|                          COOPER-HARPER HANDLING QUALITIES RATING (HQR) SUMMARY                    |`  
`+-------+-------------------+--------------------------------------------------+--------------------+`  
`| Rating| Aircraft State    | Pilot Workload Demand                            | Performance Level  |`  
`+-------+-------------------+--------------------------------------------------+--------------------+`  
`| **1-3**| Satisfactory     | Minimal pilot compensation required               | Desired Level 1    |`  
`| **4-6**| Deficiencies Exist| Moderate to extensive compensation required       | Adequate Level 2   |`  
`| **7-9**| Major Deficiencies| Maximum tolerable compensation / near loss of control| Inadequate Level 3 |`  
`| **10** | Uncontrollable    | Control lost during required operation           | Mandatory Review   |`  
`+-------+-------------------+--------------------------------------------------+--------------------+`

### **4.1 Mission Task Element (MTE) Evaluation Standards**

Flight maneuvers (e.g., sidestep, quick stop, confined area landing) are graded against strict performance boundaries:

| Mission Task Element | Desired Performance (Level 1\) | Adequate Performance (Level 2\) |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Hover Height Maintenance** | Height \\pm 2.5\\text{ ft}, Heading \\pm 5^\\circ | Height \\pm 5.0\\text{ ft}, Heading \\pm 10^\\circ |
| **Track Line Alignment** | Lateral deviation \\le \\pm 3.0\\text{ ft} | Lateral deviation \\le \\pm 6.0\\text{ ft} |
| **Confined LZ Landing** | Touchdown within 3\\text{ ft} of target mark | Touchdown within 6\\text{ ft} of target mark |
| **Autorotation Touchdown** | Airspeed 0\\text{ kts}-5\\text{ kts}, V/S \\le 200\\text{ fpm} | Airspeed 0\\text{ kts}-10\\text{ kts}, V/S \\le 400\\text{ fpm} |
